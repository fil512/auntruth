Notes:
Came to USA in 1900. Worked as railway employee until he came to Meadows in 1904 to be farm hand on 240 acre farm for William Herriot. Married Johanna Hagborg (nee Wall) in 1907. Through the years more land was added to the original acreage bought by the Hagborgs. Visited Sweden in 1919.
