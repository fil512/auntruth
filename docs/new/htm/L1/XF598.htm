Notes:
After completing school tried various jobs in Winnipeg. Married in 1962. Built home in 1963 on land that Walter had bought from uncle Victor Hagborg. Started farming in 1965 renting from Uncle Victor. In 1967 bought dad Harry's farm. Also does carpentry and floor covering. Farm was 1150 acres by 1991 when they sold on 1 April with keeping homestead house rent-free for 5 years.
