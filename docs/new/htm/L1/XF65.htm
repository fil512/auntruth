Notes:
Johanna was the fourth of seven sisters. Her sisters were: Karna - crippled at age of 18. She spent most of her life in the hospital. She lived to be quite old. Maria - Married Peter Nordin. They came to Canada and settled at Teulon. They had 8 children: Vera, Edwin, Clarence, Henry, Helmer, Margot, Victor and Arthur. Anna - married ? Sveinson. They had four children: Ingeborg, Eva, Gulli and Tore. Matilda - came to Canada married to ? Johnson. They had three children: Arthur, Signe, & Anna. Augusta - came to Canada and died very young. Kjestin - married to ? Goranson, had two sons Nils and Harold. Johanna came to Canada at age 16. Visited Sweden with Carlson daughters Doris and Edith in 1919.
