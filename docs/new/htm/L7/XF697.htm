Notes:
Adopted as indentured servant by the "Grov" when he was 8 years old. Well-educated up to age 17-18. Was scheduled to marry the wealthy Grov's daughter but the Grov died when John was in the army. During 1916 famine he built a water-wheel flour mill and was quite successful. Went to USA and took sister Maryanna who stayed.
