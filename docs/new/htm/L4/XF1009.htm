Notes:
Of Lowthorpe. Early in the sixteenth century he lived in Cherry Burton, four miles from Lowthorpe. In the thirty-seventh year of the reign of Henry VIII (1545) he appeared on the Yorkshire Subsidy Roll, assessed twice as much as any other inhabitant in the parish.
