Notes:
Born in Cherry Burton. Married Mrs. Elizabeth Clark, by whom he had eight children; she was buried in Etton 29 July 1574. His second wife Mary was buried in Etton 6 January 1588. He married third wife Jane by whom he had nine children. He moved to Etton, Harthill Wapentake, East Riding, about 1576 and made his will 5 October 1606 which was proved in January 1607.
