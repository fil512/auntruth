Notes:
Worked at Pinawa for many years. Sheet-Metal specialist. Fan and supporter of hockey. Served in WWII in Welland & Lincoln unit. Opened Beausejour Sheet Metalin late 1940s. Retired from Atomic Energy Canada(Pinawa) in 1980.Was a Town Councillor and on Agassiz School Board. Also in Lions, Foresters and Canadian Legion. Hockey/baseball player and active in community sports development. Was instrumental in establishing Beausejour J. B Comets hockey team.
