Notes:
In 1945 Arthur and wife Dorothy took over the dairy from August Selch. In 1948 they started using a milking machine powered by a gasoline motor. In 1952 when electric power became available the machine was converted to an electric unit. August Arthur sold the dairy herd in 1963. Sept 1 2002 - whilst visiting Beausejour Cemetery, Dave Hagborg was pushing Art in a wheelchair. The ground was rough and the chair almost upset. Art said 'Hey, Dave, you have to dig the hole first before you throw me out!"
